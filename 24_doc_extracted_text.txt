Welcome to the beautiful Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region. Guangxi is a multi-ethnic area with a rich and diverse ethnic composition. Here, 12 ethnic groups including the Han, Zhuang, Yao, Miao, Dong, Mulao, Maonan, Hui, Jing, Yi, Shui, and Gelao have lived in harmony for generations. The Zhuang ethnic group is the largest ethnic minority in Guangxi and is mainly distributed in Nanning, Liuzhou, Baise, Hechi, Laibin and other places. The Zhuang people have a long history and splendid culture. The literature of the Zhuang ethnic group is most famous for folk stories and ballads, such as the epic "Buluotuo", which shows the wisdom and imagination of the Zhuang people. In terms of art and literature, the song fair culture of the Zhuang ethnic group has a long history. On festivals, people gather to sing antiphonal songs with melodious voices. The copper drum culture of the Zhuang ethnic group is also a major feature. The copper drum is not only a musical instrument but also a symbol of power and wealth. The traditional craft of the Zhuang ethnic group is the Zhuang brocade, which is colorful and has exquisite patterns, and is an artistic treasure of the Zhuang people. In terms of sports, the board shoe races and throwing embroidered balls of the Zhuang ethnic group are full of fun and competitiveness. The architectural style of the Zhuang ethnic group is unique. The stilted building is a typical representative, which adapts to the humid climate in the south. The folk customs of the Zhuang ethnic group are rich and diverse. For example, on the Third of March Song Fair Festival, people will dress up in finery, sing and dance, and worship their ancestors. Traditional foods include five-color glutinous rice and bamboo tube rice, which are delicious. The Yao ethnic group is mainly distributed in Jinxiu, Du'an and other places in Guangxi. The literary works of the Yao ethnic group are mainly mythical tales and ballads, reflecting the life and beliefs of the Yao people. The long drum dance of the Yao ethnic group is the representative dance, with beautiful dance postures and a strong sense of rhythm. The cross-stitch embroidery of the Yao ethnic group is exquisite with beautiful patterns. In terms of sports, the top spinning of the Yao ethnic group is deeply loved by people. The buildings of the Yao ethnic group are mostly stilted buildings built along the mountains. In terms of folk customs, the Panwang Festival is the most important festival of the Yao ethnic group, and people will hold grand sacrificial activities. The traditional diet of the Yao ethnic group includes oil tea, etc. The Miao ethnic group mainly lives in Rongshui, Longlin and other places. The Lusheng culture of the Miao ethnic group is unique. The Lusheng is not only a musical instrument but also an important symbol of Miao culture. The embroidery of the Miao ethnic group is extremely exquisite with diverse patterns. The traditional festivals of the Miao ethnic group include the Miao New Year and the Lusheng Festival. During the festivals, people sing and dance, making it very lively. The traditional diet of the Miao ethnic group includes sour soup fish, etc. The Dong ethnic group is mainly distributed in Sanjiang and Rongshui and other places. The Dong Grand Song is a treasure of Dong culture and is famous worldwide for its multi-part, unconducted and unaccompanied singing style. The wind and rain bridges and drum towers of the Dong ethnic group have unique architectural styles and are the crystallization of the wisdom of the Dong people. The traditional festival of the Dong ethnic group is the Firecracker Festival. The traditional diet of the Dong ethnic group includes pickled fish and pickled meat. The Mulao ethnic group mainly lives in Luocheng and other places. The Yifan Festival of the Mulao ethnic group is its most important festival with a strong religious color. The production process of the coal-sand pot of the Mulao ethnic group is unique. The Maonan ethnic group mainly lives in Huanjiang and other places. The Fenlong Festival of the Maonan ethnic group is its grand festival. The bamboo hat weaving of the Maonan ethnic group is exquisite. The Hui ethnic group also has a certain distribution in Guangxi, and their religious beliefs and cultural traditions have unique characteristics. The Jing ethnic group is mainly distributed in Dongxing and other places. The solo string instrument performance of the Jing ethnic group is its unique art form. The Ha Festival of the Jing ethnic group is its important festival. The Yi ethnic group is mainly distributed in Longlin and other places. The Torch Festival of the Yi ethnic group is its famous festival. The Shui ethnic group mainly lives in Nandan and other places.
