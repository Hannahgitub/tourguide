01历史古迹
一、通用万能句框架
1.开场引入（Opening & Hook）
核心功能：吸引考官注意力，自然引出景点名称，建立专业形象。
经典问候+悬念引入：
"Ladies and gentlemen , welcome to [景点名称] . Before we step into this magnificent site , let me ask you a question: How did ancient people manage to build such a wonder without modern technology? Today , we are going to uncover the secrets behind it . "
时空穿越式引入：
"Imagine stepping back into the [朝代/世纪] century. Right here , where we stand , was once the heart of [历史事件/功能] . Welcome to  [景点名称] , a living fossil of Chinese history. "
价值定调式引入：
"What you are about to see is not just a pile of stones , but a masterpiece that has witnessed over [数字] years of glory and decline . This is  [景点名称] , a UNESCO World Heritage Site since  [年份] . "
2.历史背景（Historical Background）
核心功能：交代“Who , When , Why ”，展现逻辑叙事能力，避免枯燥背诵年份。
建造缘起（The Origin） ：
"The construction of this site was initiated by Emperor  [皇帝名] in the year [年份] . The primary purpose was to  [核心功能 ， 如： defend against invasions / honor the ancestors / serve as a summer retreat] . "
历史变迁（The Evolution） ：
"Throughout history , this site has undergone several renovations . While     the original structure dates back to the  [朝代] Dynasty , most of what you     see today was rebuilt during the  [朝代] Dynasty after a devastating fire . "
关键事件/人物（ Key Highlights） ：
" It is worth mentioning that  [历史人物] once stayed here and composed  the famous poem ' [诗句英文翻译] ' . This adds a layer of literary charm to its historical weight . "
过渡句（Transition）：
" History gives this place its soul , but architecture gives it its body. Now , let ’s shift our focus to its unique architectural features . "
3.建筑特色（Architectural Features）
核心功能：使用专业术语，结合“由远及近”或“由外到内”的游览动线，展现空间描
述能力。
整体布局（Layout & Structure）
"The entire complex follows a strict symmetrical axis (中轴线) , reflecting the traditional Chinese philosophy of harmony and order . "
"It is laid out in a courtyard style (四合院式布局) , with the main hall sitting at the center and wings extending to the sides . "
核心构件（Key Components）
屋顶：" Look up at the roof. The curved eaves (飞檐) are not just for aesthetics; they allow more sunlight in winter while blocking the scorching sun in summer . The glazed tiles (琉璃瓦) in yellow symbolize imperial power .
木结构： "What ’s amazing is that the main hall is built entirely of wood ,  held together by dougong brackets (斗拱) without a single nail. This flexible structure makes it earthquake - resistant . "
装饰细节：" Notice the carvings on the beams and pillars . Every pattern ,   whether it’s dragons , phoenixes , or peonies , carries auspicious meanings like prosperity and longevity. "
色彩与风水（Color & Feng Shui）
"The color scheme is strictly regulated: red walls represent happiness and solemnity , while yellow tiles were exclusively reserved for the
emperor .
"The site is positioned according to Feng Shui principles , with a hill at the back and water in the front , ensuring a perfect balance of Yin and Yang. "
4.文化价值与升华（ Cultural Significance & Closing）
核心功能：从“物”上升到“道”，展现思想深度，给考官留下深刻印象。
哲学/审美价值：
"This architecture is a perfect embodiment of the Chinese aesthetic: subtle yet grand (含蓄而宏大) . It teaches us the importance of living in harmony with nature . "
历史见证：
"More than just a building , i t is a silent historian . Every brick tells a story of the craftsmanship and wisdom of our ancestors . "
互动式结尾：
"As we conclude this part of the tour , I ’d like to leave you with a thought: While empires rise and fall , these stones remain . What do you      think is the most impressive part of this site? I ’d love to hear your
thoughts . "
标准结束语：
"Thank you for your attention . I hope this brief introduction has brought the history of  [景点名称] to life . Now , please feel free to take photos or ask me any questions . "
面试高分Tips（万能框架使用指南）
1.关键词替换法：
遇到宫殿/庙宇：强调Symmetry (对称) , Imperial Power (皇权) , Dougong (斗拱) .
遇到园林：强调Harmony with Nature (天人合一) , Borrowed Scenery (借景) ,  Winding Paths (曲径通幽) .
遇到防御工程（如长城）： 强调 Strategic Location (战略位置) , Military Wisdom (军事智慧), Perseverance (坚韧精神) .
2.肢体语言配合：
讲到Look up 时抬头并手势引导；
讲到Symmetry 时双手做平衡手势；
讲到Silent historian 时语气放缓，眼神真诚 。
3.应对卡壳：
如果忘了具体年份，用模糊表达："Back in the Ming Dynasty..."或"Centuries ago...
如果忘了专业词汇，用描述性语言："The wooden blocks that support the roof. . . " (代替 Dougong) .
4.逻辑连接词：
多用Speaking of..., Moving on to..., What ’s more... , Interestingly , ...让框架听起来像自然对话 ， 而不是背书 。
二、万能例句
标准欢迎：Ladies and gentlemen , welcome to  [景点名称] . It ’s my great honor to be your guide today.
（女士们先生们，欢迎来到[景点名称]。很荣幸今天能担任大家的导游。）
定调引入： What you are about to see is not just a historical site , but a living history book.
（大家即将看到的不仅仅是一处历史遗迹，更是一部活着的历史书。）
建造时间与朝代：[景点名称] was first built in the [朝代] Dynasty , with a history of over [数字] years .
（[景点名称]始建于[朝代]，距今已有[数字]多年的历史。）
建造目的： It was originally constructed for  [建造目的 ， 如： imperial defense / religious ceremonies] .
（它最初是为[皇家防御/宗教仪式]而建造的。）
历史地位： As a UNESCO World Heritage Site , it perfectly reflects the wisdom of ancient Chinese people .
（作为联合国教科文组织世界遗产，它完美体现了中国古代人民的智慧。）
整体布局：The entire complex follows a strict symmetrical axis , which represents harmony and order .
（整个建筑群遵循严格的中轴对称，代表着和谐与秩序。）
核心构件（如斗拱/飞檐） ： Look up at the roof. The curved eaves and the wooden brackets are not only for aesthetics , but also make the building earthquake - resistant .
（请看屋顶。飞檐和木斗拱不仅为了美观，还使建筑具有抗震功能 。）
色彩与装饰：Notice the red walls and yellow glazed tiles . In ancient China , these colors were exclusively reserved for the emperor .
（请注意红墙黄瓦。在古代中国，这些颜色是皇帝专属的。）
工艺赞美：Every detail , from the stone carvings to the wooden pillars , showcases the amazing craftsmanship of that era .
（从石雕到木柱，每一个细节都展示了那个时代惊人的工艺水平。）
引导视线：Now , please follow me as we move on to the main hall.
（现在，请跟随我前往主殿。）
引导观察：Dear friends , have you noticed the special patterns on the ground？
（朋友们，你们注意到地面上特殊的图案了吗？）
文化升华：More than just a building , i t is a silent historian that tells us stories of the past .
（它不仅仅是一座建筑，更是一位向我们讲述过去故事的无声历史学家。）
标准结语：Our tour of this area is coming to an end. Thank you for your attention , and I wish you a pleasant journey ahead!
（我们这一区域的游览即将结束。感谢大家的聆听，祝您接下来的旅途愉快！）
02自然山水
一、通用万能句框架
1.开场引入：定调与感官唤醒(Opening & Hook)
核心功能：迅速吸引考官注意力，建立“自然氧吧”的基调，引导考官进入情境。
感官代入式引入：
"Ladies and gentlemen , take a deep breath. Can you feel the fresh air?     Welcome to  [景点名称] , a natural oxygen bar that allows us to completely    escape the hustle and bustle of city life . "
画卷展开式引入：
" Imagine stepping into a traditional Chinese landscape painting. Right here , where we stand , is the perfect combination of wind , water , and mountains . Welcome to [景点名称] . "
价值定调式引入：
"What you are about to see is not just a scenic spot , but a masterpiece created by Mother Nature over millions of years . This is  [景点名称] , a breathtaking natural wonder . "
2.整体地貌与山水格局 (Landscape & Layout)
核心功能：交代地理特征，从宏观视角勾勒景区的整体轮廓，展现空间描述能力。
地理位置与地貌：
" Nestled in the mountains , this area features a typical  [地貌类型 ， 如： Karst / Danxia] landform , with continuous green peak s and crystal-clear waters . "
山水交融的意境：
"The scenery here is a perfect harmony of mountains and rivers . The water acts like a green silk ribbon , winding through the towering peaks . "
视觉动线引导：
"As we move along the winding paths , you will find that every turn brings new surprises . It is truly a feast for the eyes . "
3.核心自然景观拆解 (Core Natural Features)
核心功能：运用“由远及近”或“移步换景”的逻辑，结合专业词汇与生动比喻，描绘具体景观。
（1）描写水景（湖泊/江河/瀑布）
"The water here is as clear as a mirror , perfectly reflecting the blue sky      and the surrounding mountains . "
" Listen to the roaring sound of the waterfall. It plunges down from the cliff , creating a magnificent and refreshing sight . "
（2）描写山景（奇峰/怪石/森林）
" Look at those bizarre rocks and strange pines . They take various shapes ,  some resembling animals and some looking like figures , showcasing the    magic of nature . "
"The lush green forests cover the mountains , providing a tranquil habitat      for countless rare birds and animals . "
（3）描写气象（云海/日出/四季）
"Whenever it clears up after rain , a vast sea of clouds will surge among the peaks , making the whole environment dreamy and illusory. "
" Each season paints a different picture here . In spring , flowers bloom          everywhere , while in autumn , the leaves turn golden and red. "
4.游览体验与互动引导 (Experience & Interaction)
核心功能：打破单向输出，通过提问、提示和动作引导，展现导游的服务意识与控场能力。
引导视线与拍照：
" Look up at the magnificent view over there! Would you like me to take a photo of you with the sunset in the background? "
安全与行程提示：
" Now , please follow me as we head to the best sightseeing spot . Please watch your step as the mountain roads can be a bit rugged. "
分享身心感受：
"Walking along the quiet path s and listening to the singing of birds gives me a sense of tranquility that I rarely experience in daily life . How do you feel about it? "
5.结尾升华与告别 (Closing & Significance)
核心功能：从“自然之美”上升到“人与自然和谐共处”的哲学高度，留下深刻印象。
自然哲理升华：
" Standing here , looking at the boundless landscape , one can 't help but sigh at the greatness of nature . It reminds us to live in harmony with the environment and cherish our planet . "
互动式结尾：
"As we conclude this part of the tour , I ’d like to leave you with a thought : Which part of this natural wonder impressed you the most? I ’d love to       hear your thoughts . "
标准结束语：
" Our tour of this scenic area is coming to an end. I hope you have left with beautiful memories and relaxing vibes . Thank you for your cooperation , and wish you a pleasant journey ahead! "
面试高分实战Tips（万能框架使用指南）
关键词替换法：
遇到名山大川（如黄山 、泰山 ）： 强调 Majestic peaks (雄伟的山峰) , Sea of      clouds (云海) , Bizarre rocks (怪石) .
遇到江南水乡/湖泊（如西湖 、漓江） ： 强调 Tranquil water (宁静的水) , Winding paths (蜿蜒的小路) , Harmony (和谐) .
遇到森林/峡谷（如张家界 、九寨沟 ）： 强调 Lush forests (茂密的森林) , Crystal - clear waters (清澈见底的水) , Natural oxygen bar (天然氧吧) .
肢体语言配合：
讲到Take a deep breath 时 ， 做深呼吸动作 ；
讲到Look up 时 ， 抬头并用手势指向斜上方 ；
讲到Watch your step 时 ， 微微低头看路 ， 展现真实带团感 。
应对卡壳的“急救包”：
如果忘了具体的地质学词汇（如喀斯特地貌 Karst landform） ， 直接用 breathtaking landscape (令人叹为观止的景色) 或 picture -perfect view (如画般的美景) 等万能高级词汇平滑过渡 。
如果忘了具体数据， 用 millions of years (数百万年) 或 countless (无数的) 等模糊表达代替。
二、万能例句
地理位置与地貌：[景点名称] is nestled in the mountains , featuring a typical    Danxia landform with continuous green peak s and crystal-clear waters .
（[景点名称]坐落于群山之中，拥有典型的丹霞地貌，青山连绵，江水清澈见底 。）
山水交融的意境：The combination of wind , sand , and water here is truly fascinating.The scenery looks like a picture -perfect view from a traditional Chinese painting.
（这里的风、沙、水结合得十分迷人。眼前的景色宛如一幅完美的中国传统山水画。）
视觉盛宴： As we move along , you will find that every turn brings new  surprises . It is truly a feast for the eyes .
（随着我们前行，你会发现每一次转弯都带来新的惊喜。这真是一场视觉盛宴。）
描写水景（湖泊/江河）：The water here is as clear as a mirror , perfectly reflecting the blue sky and the surrounding mountains . Drifting down the river feels as if you are stepping into a fairyland.
（这里的水清澈如镜，完美地倒映着蓝天和周围的群山。顺流而下，仿佛步入了人间仙境。）
描写山景（奇峰/怪石） ： Look at those bizarre rocks and strange pines . They take various shapes , some resembling animals and some looking like figures , showcasing the magic of nature .
（看看那些怪石和奇松。它们形态各异，有的像动物，有的像人物，展现了大自然的神奇。）
描写气象（云海/日出 ） ： Whenever it clears up after rain , a vast sea of clouds will surge among the peaks . At sunrise , the sky turns pink and orange ,making the whole environment dreamy and illusory.
（每当雨后初晴，浩瀚的云海便在山峰间奔涌。日出时分，天空变成粉橙色，让整个环境如梦如幻。）
引导视线与拍照： Look up at the magnificent view over there! Would you like me to take a photo of you with the sunset in the background?
（快看那边壮丽的景色！需要我以日落为背景帮你们拍张照吗？）
分享身心感受： Walking along the quiet path s and listening to the singing of birds gives me a sense of tranquility that I rarely experience in daily life .
（沿着安静的小路漫步，聆听鸟儿的歌声，让我感受到日常生活中难得的宁静。）
03民宿古镇
一、通用万能句框架
1.开场引入：定调与情感共鸣 (Opening & Hook)
核心功能：打破传统景点的“观光感”，营造“归家”与“慢生活”的温馨氛围，迅速拉近与考官的心理距离。
时光穿越式引入：
" Ladies and gentlemen , welcome to  [古镇名称] . As we step onto these ancient cobblestone paths , it feels as if we have traveled back in time .Leave the hustle and bustle behind , and let ’s embrace the slow pace of     life here . "
（女士们先生们，欢迎来到[古镇名称]。当我们踏上这些古老的鹅卵石小路时，仿佛穿越回了过去。把城市的喧嚣抛在脑后，让我们在这里拥抱慢节奏的生活。）
" Imagine a traditional Chinese ink wash painting coming to life . Welcome to [古镇名称] , a place where white walls , dark tiles , and winding waterways tell stories of a thousand years . "
（想象一幅中国传统水墨画跃然眼前。欢迎来到[古镇名称]，在这里，粉墙黛瓦和蜿蜒的水道诉说着千年的故事。）
文化定调式引入：
"What you are about to experience is not just a tourist attraction , but a living museum of traditional Chinese residential culture . This is  [古镇名称] , a perfect blend of history and modern hospitality. "
（大家即将体验的不仅仅是一个旅游景点，更是一座中国传统居住文化的活态博物馆。这就是[古镇名称]，历史与现代待客之道的完美交融。）
2.古镇风貌与历史底蕴 (Town Landscape & History)
核心功能：勾勒古镇的宏观轮廓，交代历史脉络，展现对地域文化的深刻理解。
地理与格局：
" Nestled in the south/north of  [省份] , this ancient town is built along the river . The layout perfectly reflects the ancient Chinese philosophy of living    in harmony with nature and water . "
（这座古镇坐落于[省份]的南部/北部，依水而建。其布局完美体现了中国古代人与自然、水和谐共生的哲学理念。）
历史变迁：
"With a history of over  [数字] years , this town used to be a bustling hub for merchants and scholars . Although time has passed , the original architecture has    been well preserved. "
（这座古镇拥有[数字]多年的历史，曾是商贾和文人墨客云集的繁华枢纽。尽管时光流逝，原始的建筑风貌依然得到了很好的保护。）
过渡到民宿：
"While the ancient streets carry the weight of history , the unique homestays  (民宿) here inject new vitality into this old town .  Let ’s take a closer look at how history meets modern comfort . "
（古老的街道承载着历史的厚重，而这里独特的民宿则为这座古镇注入了新的活力。让我们近距离看看历史是如何与现代舒适感相遇的。）
3.核心亮点：特色民宿体验 (Core Highlight: Homestay Experience)
核心功能：这是本主题的重头戏。通过描述建筑改造、内部设施和文化体验，展现“宿在风景里”的独特魅力。
(1)建筑改造与外观 (Architecture & Renovation)
"These homestays are carefully renovated from traditional courtyards . They keep the original wooden structure and carved windows , while adding modern amenities like heating and private bathrooms . "
（这些民宿都是由传统庭院精心改造而成的。它们保留了原有的木质结构和雕花窗棂，同时增加了供暖和独立卫浴等现代化设施。）
" Notice the courtyard design . It provides a private and tranquil space       where you can enjoy a cup of tea and watch the stars at night . "
（请注意庭院的设计。它提供了一个私密宁静的空间，您可以在这里品一杯清茶，夜晚仰望星空。）
(2)沉浸式文化体验 (Immersive Cultural Experience)
" Staying here is more than just sleeping. You can experience traditional     local crafts , such as tie -dyeing (扎染) or making rice cakes , right in the     homestay. "
（住在这里不仅仅是为了睡觉。您甚至可以在民宿里直接体验当地的传统手工艺，比如扎染或制作米糕。）
"The hosts are usually local residents . Over a homemade breakfast , they will share fascinating folk tales and hidden gems of t he town that you won 't find in guidebooks . "
（民宿主人通常是当地居民。在享用自制早餐时，他们会分享引人入胜的民间故事，以及您在旅游指南上找不到的古镇隐藏宝藏。）
4.游览互动与感官引导 (Interaction & Sensory Guidance)
核心功能：调动考官的视觉、听觉和触觉，增强讲解的画面感和代入感。
引导视线与细节：
" Look at the moss on the stone stairs and the red lanterns hanging under    the eaves . Every detail here is a perfect photo opportunity. "
（看看石阶上的青苔和屋檐下悬挂的红灯笼。这里的每一个细节都是绝佳的拍照机会。）
安全与行程提示：
" Now , please follow me as we explore the next alley. The stone paths can be a bit slippery , especially after rain , so please watch your step. "
（现在，请跟随我探索下一条小巷。石板路可能会有些湿滑，尤其是雨后，请大家注意脚下安全。）
情感互动：
" Listening to the gentle sound of the flowing water , do you feel a sense of inner peace? This is exactly what the locals call 'the poetry of daily life ' . "
（听着潺潺的流水声，您是否感受到了一种内心的宁静？这正是当地人所说的“日常生活的诗意”。）
5.结尾升华与告别 (Closing & Significance)
核心功能：从“住宿体验”升华到“文化传承”，强调民宿在保护古镇文化中的积极作用。
文化传承升华：
"These homestays are not just places to stay; they are the guardians o f our cultural heritage . They allow ancient architecture to breathe again and let   travelers truly connect with the local soul. "
（这些民宿不仅仅是住宿的地方，它们更是我们文化遗产的守护者。它们让古老的建筑重新焕发生机，让旅行者真正与当地的灵魂产生共鸣。）
互动式结尾：
"As our tour comes to an end , I ’d love to ask: If you had a weekend to escape the city , would you choose to stay in a homestay like this? I ’d love to hear your thoughts . "
（随着我们的游览接近尾声，我想问大家一个问题：如果您有一个周末可以逃离城市，您会选择住在这样的民宿里吗？我很想听听您的想法。）
标准结束语：
"Thank you for walking through this ancient town with me today. I hope you take home not just photos , but a piece of this peaceful lifestyle . Wish you a wonderful journey ahead! "
（感谢大家今天与我一同漫步这座古镇。希望大家带走的不仅是照片，还有这份宁静生活方式的印记。祝大家接下来的旅途愉快！）
面试高分实战Tips（万能框架使用指南）
关键词替换法：

06突发情况应对
一、万能答题逻辑框架(Universal Answering Framework)
核心功能：在面试中遇到任何突发状况题，都能按照“安抚情绪-采取行动-后续跟进”的逻辑条理清晰地作答，展现专业素养。
第一步：安抚情绪与评估状况 (Calm Down & Assess)
" First and foremost , I will stay calm and reassure the tourists . I need to     quickly assess the situation to ensure everyone 's safety. "
（首先，我会保持冷静并安抚游客的情绪。我需要迅速评估现场状况，确保所有人的安全。）
第二步：采取紧急应对措施 (Take  Immediate Action)
"Then , I will take immediate action according to the emergency procedures , such as calling for medical assistance or contacting the relevant authorities . "
（接着，我会根据应急预案采取紧急措施，比如呼叫医疗救援或联系相关官方机构。）
第三步： 后续跟进与记录  (Follow - up &  Keep  Records)
"After the situation is under control ,  I will keep a detailed record of the incident  and write a re port for the travel agency to ensure proper follow - up . "
（在局势得到控制后，我会做好详细的记录，并向旅行社提交书面报告，以确保后续的妥善处理。）
二、高频突发场景与实战话术 (High-Frequency Scenarios & Practical Expressions)
核心功能：针对面试中最常考的几类突发事件，提供直接可以背诵的英文应对策略。
1.游客走失(Tourist Getting  Lost)
应对策略：
" If a tourist gets lost ,  I will first count the number of people and confirm the        missing person ' s features . Then ,  I will initiate emergency procedures , such as      broadcasting a search message or contacting the local police . "
（如果有游客走失，我会首先清点人数并确认走失者的特征。然后，启动应急预案，如广播寻人或联系当地警方。）
现场沟通：
" Please remain calm , everyone . We are initiating emergency procedures to find your friend. "
（请大家保持冷静。我们正在启动紧急预案寻找您的朋友。）
2.突发疾病或中暑(Sudden Illness or Heatstroke)
应对策略：
"In case of a medical emergency like heatstroke,I will immediately movethe tourist to a shady and well-ventilated area.I will provide first aid with acold towel and call for medical assistance right away."
(如果遇到中暑等突发疾病，我会立即将游客转移到阴凉通风处。用冷毛巾提供急救，并立刻呼叫医疗救援。)
现场沟通：
"We need to move to a shady area immediately.Please wait here,I'm calling for medical assistance."
(我们需要立刻转移到阴凉处。请在这里稍等，我正在呼叫医疗救援。)
3.游客物品遗失(Lost Property/Luggage)
应对策略：
"I will help the tourist retrace their steps and search carefully.If the item cannot be found,I will help them get a certificate of loss from the local police station and assist with insurance reimbursement."
(我会帮助游客原路返回并仔细寻找。如果找不到，我会协助他们从当地派出所开具遗失证明，并协助办理保险理赔。)
现场沟通：
"Don't worry.Let's retrace your steps first.I will help you get a certificate of loss if necessary."
(别担心。让我们先原路返回找找。如有必要，我会帮您开具遗失证明。)
4.景点突发关闭或行程变更(Attraction Closure /Itinerary Change)应对策略：
"I will get the official notice immediately and explain the situation to the tourists.To win their understanding,I will quickly adjust the itinerary and provide an exciting alternative tour."
(我会第一时间获取官方通知并向游客解释情况。为了争取他们的谅解，我会迅速调整行程，并提供一个令人兴奋的备选游览方案。)
现场沟通：
"I'm sorry for the inconvenience,but the attraction is temporarily closed.I've prepared an exciting alternative tour for us."
(很抱歉带来不便，该景点暂时关闭。我已经为大家准备了一个精彩的备选行程。)
三、考场“急救包”：忘词与听不懂怎么办？（Emergency Kit For the Exam）
核心功能：在面试高压环境下，用礼貌且专业的英语为自己争取思考时间，避免冷场
没听清考官的问题：
"I'm sorry,I didn't catch that.Could you please repeat the question?"(不好意思，我没听清，能请您重复一遍问题吗?)
突然忘词或卡壳：
"The word is on the tip of my tongue.Let me explain it in another way..."(这个词就在嘴边，让我换种方式解释一下……)
遇到完全不会的极端问题：
"That's a tricky question. I'm not entirely familiar with this specific case,but I would prioritize the tourists' safety and follow the agency's guidelines."
(这个问题有点棘手。我对这个特定案例不太熟悉，但我会把游客的安全放在首位，并遵循旅行社的指导方针。)
四、面试高分实战Tips(应变能力备考指南)
核心词汇必须脱口而出：
考前务必熟记这些高频词：First aid(急救),Food poisoning(食物中毒),PublicSecurity Bureau(公安局),Insurance(保险),Reimbursement(赔偿),Safetyprecautions(安全提示).
展现“服务意识”大于“标准答案”:
考官不仅看你的英语，更看你的态度。在回答任何突发情况时，一定要加上comfortthe tourists(安抚游客),keep a record(做好记录),report to the travel agency(向旅行社汇报)这三个动作，这是导游业务规范的得分点。
肢体语言与眼神交流：
回答应急问题时，眼神要坚定，语速可以适当放慢，表现出你在“模拟真实带团”时的沉稳。讲到call for medical assistance时，可以做一个拿手机拨打电话的微小手势，增强画面感。
